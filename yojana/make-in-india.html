Make in India

Ministry: Commerce Category: Manufacturing Launch: Sep 2014

Overview

Make in India - Promote manufacturing in India. This scheme is run by the Government of India to provide financial and social assistance to deserving citizens.

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive various types of support from the government, helping them improve their standard of living and become self-reliant.

Benefits

  • Main Benefit: Promote manufacturing in India
  • Government Support: Direct Benefit Transfer
  • Easy Process: Minimum documents required
  • Online Tracking: Check status from home
  • Helpline Support: 1800-11-5656

Eligibility

  • Must be an Indian citizen
  • Age and income limits as per scheme
  • Aadhaar Card required
  • Bank Account required for DBT
  • Scheme-specific conditions apply

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Account Passbook
  • Address Proof
  • Income Certificate
  • Passport size Photo
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply

  1. Visit official website https://makeinindia.com
  2. Click on "Apply" or "Registration"
  3. Fill in your details (Name, Aadhaar, Mobile)
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Submit and save your reference number
  6. Or visit nearest CSC center / office

Official Website

https://makeinindia.com

Helpline: 1800-11-5656

FAQ

Q: Am I eligible?

A: Check eligibility on the official website. Generally Indian citizens within income limits are eligible.

Q: Is applying free?

A: Yes, applying is completely free.

Q: How long does approval take?

A: Usually 15-30 days for verification and approval.

Q: How to check status?

A: Use "Track Application" section on the official website.

Q: How is money paid?

A: Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) to your bank account.

Q: Who can help me?

A: Call helpline 1800-11-5656 or visit nearest CSC center.
